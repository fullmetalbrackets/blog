The original for Game Boy is one of my favorite games of all time, and this is just the perfect remake that leaves the gameplay totally intact while redoing the graphics in this beautiful figurine-like art style that really keeps the vibe of the original dot matrix graphics. Amazing. (Also this released on my birthday, which is pretty cool.)
