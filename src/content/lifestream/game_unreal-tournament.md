This is my favorite FPS ever, I played the hell out of it from around 1999 to 2001, when they started coming out with the sequels and my computer could no longer play it. (Nor could I afford an upgrade as a 19 year old.)

There are still a few custom servers that get actual human players on the weekends, but for the most part this game is long dead, unfortunately.
