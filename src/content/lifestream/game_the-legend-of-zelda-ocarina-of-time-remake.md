Masterful remake of one of the best video games ever made. It looks and sounds incredible, anyone saying this looks like an Unreal Engine fangame hasn't played or watched past the first hour of the game, and the fully-ochestral versions of music from the original really hit in the feels.

I didn't 100% it this time, but I will on next playthrough in a year or so. (I never 100% on the original either.)
