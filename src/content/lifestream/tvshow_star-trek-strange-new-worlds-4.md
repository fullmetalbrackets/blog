I love this show, but very conflicted about this season. Almost every episode has some gimmick, but only a few of them are any good. The first three episodes were okay, but it went off the rails after that. The soap opera episode was surprisingly good (Anson Mount can fucking act, I got teary-eyed) and so was the season finale.
