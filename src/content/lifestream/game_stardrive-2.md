Rather than keep the unique real-time gameplay of the original and build on it, the developer decided to make a turn-based spiritual successor to Master of Orion 2... It's okay, but nowhere near as good as neither the original Master of Orion 2 or the newer Master of Orion remake.

It still feels half-baked and unpolished like the original, and exterminating everyone else still seems to be the only way to win, but at least this time the enemy A.I. is not totally brain dead.
