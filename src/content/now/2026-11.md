The wife and I went on a week-long trip to Utah, stayed at a beautiful mountain cabin, went stargazing, visited Bryce Canyon and Zion National Park. Then that whole ground stop thing happened the day we were travelling back home, our connecting flight was cancelled like every other flight in the country, and we got stuck in Dallas for a day.

It was 7 days of fun and one day of inconvenience, but that last day my wife and I were part of history. So, a memorable trip overall.

I received my physical copy of the Ocarina of Time remake the day before we left, so I played it some on the flights and especially while waiting around during the ground stop. Now that we're home I'll be really putting in some playtime, and I'm looking forward to playing it on my 4K TV instead of portably. (Although it still looks and plays incredible portable!)
